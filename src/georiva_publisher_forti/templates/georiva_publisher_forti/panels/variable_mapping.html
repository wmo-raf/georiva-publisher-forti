{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The eight slots, and the three things that can be said about one. Every distinction on this page was drawn in `forms.py` and arrives here as a row: which parameters the slot feeds, whether anything fills it, what was refused about it and what is merely suspected. Nothing is decided in an `{% if %}` here, because a distinction drawn in a template is one nobody tests — the rule `panels/publish_history.html` already follows, and for the same reason. The warnings sit **under the chooser they are about** rather than in a list at the foot of the section. A warning names a slot, and a reader who has to carry the slot name down the page to find the row it belongs to is being asked to do the join the form is for. What the form did not check is stated last and in full. It is the part most easily lost to a redesign that reads it as boilerplate, so it is prose in a bordered block rather than a muted footnote: an operator who reads a saved mapping as a verified one has been misled by this page, and no amount of correct checking above makes that better. The style block is here rather than in `{% block extra_css %}` because a panel is a fragment inside somebody else's form and has no blocks to fill; the classes are prefixed `fm-` for the same reason `fh-` is on the history panel and `fv-` on the verification one. {% endcomment %}
